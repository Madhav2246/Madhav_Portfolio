R — 02
Predicting Dominant Gamete Contribution in Fertilization Using Genomic & Phenotypic Characteristics
In Progress
Bioinformatics · Reproductive AI · Genomics
A novel hybrid AI-bioinformatics pipeline predicting whether the male or female gamete dominantly contributes to successful fertilization. Integrates WHO semen phenotypic data, SHBG codon-based synthetic SNPs, and female oocyte transcriptomic profiles (GSE107746). Gene Ontology enrichment via g:Profiler provides biological grounding. Random Forest achieves 92% accuracy (ROC-AUC 0.94). SHAP analysis reveals female gene expression — particularly BMP15, GDF9, ZP3 — as the primary dominance driver.

R — 03
Continual Learning on CIFAR-100 via Hard Attention to Task (HAT) with Memory-Aware Replay & EWC
In Progress
Continual Learning · Catastrophic Forgetting · Computer Vision
A research evolution tracking the progression from naive RoutedMLP (26% on Split-CIFAR-100) through CNN dynamic routing (41%), ResNet18 pretrained backbone (49%), to a full HAT architecture achieving 58.86% accuracy. HAT introduces task-specific sigmoid attention gates over ResNet18 features, combined with EWC regularization and episodic memory replay, enabling selective attention to task-relevant neurons and significant retention over five incremental tasks.
